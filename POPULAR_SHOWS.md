# Popular TV Shows - IMDB IDs for TurboPi

Use these IMDB IDs to search for popular shows in TurboPi.

## How to Use
1. Navigate to "TV Shows" in TurboPi
2. Enter the IMDB ID (just the numbers, without "tt") in the search box
3. Click "Search IMDB" to load all available episodes

## How to Find IMDB IDs

1. Go to [IMDb.com](https://www.imdb.com)
2. Search for your TV show
3. Look at the URL: `https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0903747/`
4. The ID is the part after `tt` - in this case: `0903747`
5. Enter just the numbers in TurboPi's search box

## Notes

- EZTV primarily focuses on recent episodes and currently airing shows

- Recent seasons of popular shows typically have the best availability
